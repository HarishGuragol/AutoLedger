Ingestion Sources & Automated Feed Setup

Direct SMS & iMessage Ingestion from Apple Messages

When your iPhone forwards bank/UPI SMS to your Mac (Settings > Messages > Text Message Forwarding), AutoLedger can automatically read incoming bank texts from the local database.

How to Enable Full Disk Access on macOS:
  1. Open System Settings on your Mac.
  2. Go to Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access.
  3. Toggle ON for your Terminal application (or Python / VS Code).
  4. Click the "Scan SMS Now" button below to test immediate import.

Automated Bank Email Transaction Ingestion

Connect your email (e.g. Gmail) via secure IMAP to scan incoming transaction receipts from HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Paytm, or CRED.

Generate an App Password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords

Real-Time iPhone & Mac Shortcut Trigger (Zero Setup)

You can set up a simple Apple Shortcut on your iPhone or Mac that immediately pushes ANY incoming bank notification or SMS straight to AutoLedger in real-time!

Local Webhook Endpoint: 
POST http://localhost:5050/api/ingest/webhook
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "text": "Paid Rs. 340 to Swiggy via UPI on 22-Nov-26 (Ref: 432190812345)",
  "source": "iPhone Shortcut"
}
How to set up on iPhone (Automations):
  1. Open the Shortcuts app on iOS and tap Automation > New Automation.
  2. Choose Message > "Message Contains" e.g. debited or Rs > Run Immediately.
  3. Add action: Get Contents of URL > http://<your-mac-ip>:5050/api/ingest/webhook > POST > Pass message text.
  4. Every single UPI transaction or ATM withdrawal automatically records itself into your tracker with zero manual taps!
